# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and precise. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing and deliberate exposure of
credentials or private session content are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove unsafe content, close discussions or restrict participation
to protect contributors. Decisions should be proportionate and explained when safe.
Appeals can be sent through the maintainer's GitHub profile; do not republish harmful content.

For vulnerabilities or credential exposure, use the private reporting path in SECURITY.md.
